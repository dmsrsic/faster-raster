# FasterRaster v1.0.0-beta.4

Beta.4 adds source-aware, index-guided hybrid classification while preserving the existing broad weak-supervised classification, deterministic acquisition, provenance, publication, and beta.3 contract-repair workflows.

## Highlights

- First-class, deterministically hashed spectral-index registry.
- Semantic source-band compatibility and fail-closed missing-band validation.
- Index discovery with `fr indices list` and `fr indices show`.
- Safe bounded custom arithmetic index expressions without arbitrary Python execution.
- Additive `AgriculturalRecipeV4` workflow.
- Explicit broad general classes plus specialist index classes.
- Single-index thresholds and bounded ranges.
- Boolean multi-index rules.
- Explicitly normalized weighted scores.
- Target spectral-signature similarity.
- User-defined specialist mode.
- Recommendation mode with a nonfinal `AWAITING_INDEX_SELECTION` review package.
- Explicitly authorized automatic selection with bounded candidate search.
- Nested spatial selection that preserves an untouched outer holdout.
- Deterministic parent-class restrictions, priorities, overlap arbitration, and uncertainty states.
- Separate general, specialist-score, specialist-candidate, final-hybrid, and decision-state rasters.
- Analytical index COGs, statistics, receipts, checksums, provenance, and `fr inspect` support.
- Deterministic 3840×2160 audit publication.
- Zero-network publication-only rerendering.
- Compatibility with beta.3 year, date-range, bounding-box, and point-buffer repair.

## Scientific boundaries

- Ordinary four-band NAIP cannot provide NDMI or NBR because it lacks SWIR bands.
- Raw NAIP digital numbers are not guaranteed surface reflectance.
- Weak-label agreement is not an independent ground-truth accuracy assessment.
- Automatically selected indices do not establish physical causation.
- Thresholds may not transfer across dates, mosaics, sensors, or radiometric products.
- A spectral target signature does not prove mining, abandonment, contamination, ownership, or safety.
